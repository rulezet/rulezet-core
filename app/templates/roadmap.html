Roadmap

Where Rulezet is going: from the place where detection rules are shared and curated, to the place where they are tested, deployed to real tools and improved by what happens in production.

The direction
Curate

Rules are shared, validated, reviewed and grouped into bundles — what Rulezet does today.

Test

The Lab proves what each rule detects on reference data, and scores it.

Deploy

Connectors deliver curated sets to the real tools, natively.

Learn

Sightings and production counters flow back and improve the rules.
