Legal Notice

Site identification, licensing, and liability information for the Rulezet platform.

1. Site Identification

Rulezet is an open-source project. The official instance is published at rulezet.org. Rulezet is also free software that anyone can deploy on their own infrastructure — a self-hosted instance is operated independently by whoever runs it, who is then the responsible publisher for that instance instead of the Rulezet project.

2. Publisher

The official instance is published and maintained by the Rulezet open-source project and its contributors, coordinated on GitHub at github.com/rulezet/rulezet-core. For formal legal correspondence regarding a specific instance, contact that instance's operator directly.

3. Hosting

Rulezet is self-hostable software; the hosting provider therefore depends on which instance you are using. Details for the official rulezet.org instance are available on request via the contact channels below.

Detection rules, bundles, and other content submitted by users remain the property of their original authors and are shared under the license each contributor selects for their own submission (shown on the rule's detail page). Rulezet itself claims no ownership over user-submitted rule content.

5. Content Disclaimer

Detection rules hosted on Rulezet are submitted by the community "as is," without warranty of any kind. Rulezet does not guarantee the accuracy, safety, or fitness for any particular purpose of any rule, and is not responsible for how rules are used. Always review and test detection content before deploying it in a production environment.

Each user is responsible for what they publish or send — rules, comments and messages. Content that is illegal, abusive or spam can be removed and its author's account suspended. To check a report of abuse, an administrator can reveal a single message; every such reveal is logged (see the Privacy Policy, “who sees what”).

6. Rule Sources, Feeds & Licences
Every rule keeps its own licence

Each rule is published under the licence chosen by its author or by the source it comes from — shown on its page, in the API and above it in every feed, together with its author and its source. Rulezet does not relicense rules: whoever reuses one must follow its licence. In particular, rules under the Detection Rule License (DRL) 1.1 require the author to be named in any copy of the rule and in any output showing its matches; rules under the GPL / AGPL keep their copyleft conditions; MIT, BSD and Apache rules keep their notices.

Rules imported from public sources

Rulezet imports rules from public repositories (Git Sources) and from the rule feeds below, only when their licence allows them to be republished. Each imported rule links back to its source and keeps its author and licence.

Removal on request

If you hold rights on a rule published here and did not allow it, or want it attributed differently, contact us (section 10) with the rule's link: it is removed or corrected promptly, and its source is not imported again.

Rulezet's feeds and API
  • The rule feeds and the API need a free account and its personal API key; keep it private.
  • A feed gives each rule with its licence, author and source: they must be kept when you redistribute it, and the rule's licence followed (attribution for DRL 1.1, copyleft for GPL…).
  • Rules marked TLP:RED or TLP:AMBER are never in a feed. TLP markings of the rules you receive must be respected.
  • Feeds are provided "as is", without any warranty (section 5) and may change or stop. Abusive use (excessive downloads, sharing a key, resale of the feed as such) may lead to the key being revoked.
  • Do not present rules from Rulezet as your own or as endorsed by Rulezet or by their authors.
