About Rulezet

An open platform where the security community shares, improves and uses detection rules — built in the open by CIRCL.

Our mission

Every detection rule, in one place — open, checked and ready to use.

Detection rules live scattered across thousands of git repositories, blogs and private folders. Rulezet brings them together, checks them, lets the community improve them and hands them to the tools that need them — YARA, Sigma, Suricata, Zeek and many more.

What Rulezet does
Three things, for detection engineers, SOC analysts and researchers.

Share

Import rules straight from git repositories (GitHub, GitLab, Gitea…), write new ones in a real editor, and keep the original author, license and source attached.

Improve

Every rule is validated. The community votes, comments, proposes edits like pull requests, reports false positives — and repositories are watched for updates.

Use

Group rules into bundles, follow a CVE or an ATT&CK technique, and pull them into your tools through the API, feeds, MISP or Velociraptor.

Who is behind it
A CIRCL project, open source and co-funded by the European Union.
CIRCL
Computer Incident Response Center Luxembourg — the national CERT, which leads and develops Rulezet, alongside tools such as MISP and vulnerability-lookup.
Luxembourg House of Cybersecurity
CIRCL is part of the LHC, Luxembourg's hub for cybersecurity.
Co-funded by the European Union
Rulezet is co-funded by CIRCL and by the European Union under the FETTA project (Federated European Team for Threat Analysis).

Our principles
What you can count on.
Open sourceRulezet is free software under the GNU AGPL v3. Run your own instance, even fully offline.
Licenses respectedEach rule keeps its own license and author, and links back to where it comes from.
Open by defaultRules are public; bundles can be private for your own team.
Sharing labelsTLP and PAP on every rule, so you know how it may be shared and used.
Community moderationAnyone can report a broken or harmful rule; admins review every report.
Full historyEvery change of a rule is kept, with who made it and why.
